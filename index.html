Everything You Need to Know

What happens after I submit my booking request?

Once you submit your request, you'll receive an email copy for your records, and a dedicated concierge personally reviews your details and reaches out via WhatsApp to finalize your itinerary, confirm availability, and answer any remaining questions. Once everything is confirmed, you'll receive a secure payment link to complete your booking.

Private experiences are secured with a 50% deposit, with the remaining balance settled securely before your experience. In-villa and dedicated-dispatch services, including Mobile Massage, Family Concierge, and Arrival Concierge, require full payment at the time of confirmation to reserve your dedicated specialists.

A note on your host: If you'd like your host to communicate in a specific language, simply mention it in your notes when booking, and we'll do our best to accommodate. Every host assignment is based on availability, language, logistics, and the best overall fit for each experience — so while your preferences are welcome, they may not always be possible.

We are a large group. Will we be transported in a minibus?

Absolutely not. Minibuses inherently compromise comfort and restrict access to Bali's more exclusive, hidden locations due to narrow infrastructure. For groups of 5 or more, your group is automatically assigned additional premium vehicles and dedicated hosts. We replace the standard group-tour model with a coordinated fleet of premium vehicles and a scaled concierge team (1 dedicated host per 4 guests). This allows your group to travel comfortably together or separate during the day whenever needed—for example, if some guests wish to return to the villa while others continue exploring.

Do infants and young children count toward the group size?

Yes. Every passenger—including infants and young children—is provided with their own dedicated seat and age-appropriate child restraint. This ensures every journey meets the same standard of comfort and safety while maintaining the four-guest capacity of each private vehicle.

Can we customize an experience?

Absolutely. Every experience is private and tailored to you, whether you're traveling solo or with a group. Before your experience, your dedicated host will finalize the itinerary with you. Once your host welcomes you, the day becomes yours. Stay longer somewhere you love, skip a stop, pause for lunch, or adjust the pace as the day unfolds—whenever practical, your host will gladly accommodate your preferences. Your itinerary is there to guide the day, never to limit it.

What if I want to explore places not included in your experiences?

Of course. Our curated experiences are simply a starting point—they're designed to inspire, not define, what's possible. If there's another destination, hidden gem, or part of Bali you'd like to explore, we'd be delighted to tailor an experience around it. Just let us know what you have in mind, and we'll handle the planning.

Can you help us during our stay?

Absolutely. Beyond your booked experience, your concierge can help coordinate the smaller details of your stay too:

Dining Restaurant and beach club reservations, sunset dining, and celebration setups for special occasions.
Transportation Airport transfers, inter-island ferries, and private drivers whenever you need to move beyond your itinerary.
Wellness In-villa massage, spa reservations, and yoga sessions arranged around your schedule.
Family Babysitting, elderly assistance, and family-friendly recommendations for every generation traveling with you.
Villa Comfort Grocery stocking, catering delivery, and thoughtful touches like flowers or decorations before you arrive.
Lifestyle Laundry service, scooter rentals, photographer recommendations, and last-minute bookings.

Don't see what you need? Ask anyway — your concierge is only a WhatsApp message away.

Do your packages cover North/East Bali?

Yes. While our primary operations are centralized in the South, we facilitate experiences island-wide. We never apply hidden location surcharges. If your accommodation requires extensive transit to reach an experience, your concierge will proactively advise you on adjustments to protect your time and comfort.

Do you offer airport transfers?

We elevate the standard airport transfer into a frictionless protocol. Whether you are booking a complete Vurata itinerary or simply require a standalone premium transition, our concierge coordinates the entire door-to-door process between Ngurah Rai International Airport and any accommodation in Bali.

For arrivals: Your dedicated host intercepts you the moment you exit the terminal, manages your luggage, and escorts you to a private, climate-controlled vehicle.

For departures: We ensure your transition from your villa or hotel back to the airport is just as secure, private, and unhurried.

Want your host and vehicle to stay with you well beyond the drive to your villa? Our Airport Concierge package extends this into a fully dedicated service for up to 8 hours after landing — including seamless villa provisioning — so your first day in Bali is handled from the moment you touch down.

Do I need to arrange my own tickets or island transfers?

Absolutely not. From tickets to transfers, every detail is arranged for you. Whether you are visiting a temple, a beach club, or taking a fast-boat to a neighboring island, your host handles all ticketing, port logistics, and transfers from start to finish. You simply enjoy the experience.

Are meals and personal purchases included?

No. To provide you with total flexibility, meals, dining, shopping, and VIP venue tables are out-of-pocket. Your host will happily provide recommendations and facilitate reservations, but your package strictly covers the stated logistics, transport, and expert curation.

What if my flight is delayed or our plans change?

We understand that travel plans can change unexpectedly. If your flight is delayed or your itinerary changes, simply let your concierge know as soon as possible. Whenever operationally possible, we'll do everything we can to adjust your arrangements and keep your experience running smoothly.

How is my personal information managed?

We treat your privacy with the exact same discretion as our physical experiences. Your contact details, preferences, and family information are used exclusively by our internal concierge team to coordinate your itinerary and ensure a seamless service. We never sell, lease, or share your data with third-party marketers.

What payment methods do you accept?

To guarantee a seamless and secure experience, we operate on a strictly cashless basis. We accept Visa, Mastercard, Wise, and direct Bank Transfers for all deposits and final balances. We do not accept cash on-site.

What is your cancellation policy?

Our policy is strictly structured based on how far in advance you secure your reservation to protect our local partners' inventory:

Booked 30+ days in advance Cancellations permitted up to 20 days from booking confirmation.
Booked 15–29 days in advance Cancellations permitted up to 7 days from booking confirmation.
Booked 4–14 days in advance Free cancellation requires at least 72 hours' notice.
Booked < 72 hours in advance A strict 12-hour grace period from the time of booking applies.
Same-Day & Mobile Services These services are strictly non-refundable once confirmed due to the immediate allocation of your dedicated specialists.

Cancellations initiated by us due to severe weather or unsafe conditions are fully refundable.

Is tipping included or expected?

Gratuity is never required but always appreciated. If your host or driver provided an exceptional experience, you are welcome to tip them directly at your discretion.
